# GitNexus benchmark forensics

The public Akon/GitNexus result is useful mechanism evidence but not a fully
reproducible causal benchmark. The published arithmetic is inconsistent: 113
tasks multiplied by three arms and ten trials is 3,390, while the page reports
3,471 trials; a visible manifest contains 116 entries, implying 3,480. The
reported rates reconstruct from 1,157 attempts per arm and 791/625/428 solves.

High-confidence source conclusions are bounded process composition,
same-observation search augmentation, and explicit ambiguity/unresolved/
external resolution states. Its public evaluator also changes prompts and
exposes graph commands, so the outcome does not isolate augmentation. Its
delivery and staleness contracts are weaker than GT's exact request, revision,
and receipt requirements.

Direct comparison requires identical task/repository SHAs, model route,
scaffold, prompts, budgets, evaluator, index configuration, and trial policy.
